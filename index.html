What you're looking at

Two tangled strands of beads, amber and teal, and a small neural network learning to tell them apart. The clear cube shows where every bead sits after each layer of the network, and the silver grid shows what that layer did to space itself.

Why it matters: neural networks don't learn rules. They learn to bend, stretch and fold space until things that belong together end up on the same side of one flat sheet. It's the same geometric idea behind how big language models organise meaning, small enough here to watch.

Try it: drag 'Walk through the layers' to watch the strands untwist one step at a time. Switch to Linked rings and see the network get stuck, then open Advanced and give it more neurons per layer: it lifts one ring out through an extra dimension. Tap any bead to follow its journey.
